Privacy

What we collect: your public TikTok handle, your nickname, the public comments you post under the series' videos, the like counts on those comments, and whether you follow @laiato.

Why: to run the game: houses, votes and rankings.

Source: public comments on @laiato's TikTok videos.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running this interactive series.

What we do not keep: no profile pictures, no private messages.

How long: as long as the series runs. Removed on request.

Opt out or get removed: comment REMOVE ME under any episode, or send a direct message to @laiato on TikTok. Removal within 7 days.

Your rights: under the GDPR you can access your data, have it erased, and object to its use. You can also complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority: https://www.cnil.fr/.

Cookies: this site sets no cookies itself. The TikTok player loads only when you click "Load the TikTok video", and TikTok may then set its own cookies.

Theme: if you pick light or dark, that choice is kept in your browser's local storage. It is not a cookie and is never sent anywhere.

Under 15: if you are under 15, ask a parent before taking part. We remove any handle on request.

Data refreshed daily.
