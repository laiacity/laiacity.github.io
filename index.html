    Deputy mayor

    • Laïa is the mayor and stays mayor. The election picks her deputy mayor. It unlocks at 100 followers.
    • To run, comment I RUN under the latest episode. The most-liked candidacy wins.
    • The deputy serves 2 episodes.
    • The deputy tips the balance in every vote during their term.
    • The deputy can pass one law of their choice, which lasts their whole term.
    • If Laïa judges that law abusive, the law's first episode opens a keep-or-repeal vote.

    Candidates are residents who commented I RUN. Sorted by likes.

      How to play

      Comment one of these formats under the episode video. Copy an example, change the words, post it.

      • Idea Build something

        The most-liked IDEA gets built.

        IDEA: a giant duck fountain

      • Law Make a rule

        The most-liked LAW applies.

        LAW: hats are mandatory

      • Destroy Demolish a target

        The most-liked target is demolished, houses included. Put the @ of the target after the colon.

        DESTROY: @handle

      • House Customise your house

        Your last HOUSE request is the one shown on your house card.

        HOUSE: red roof, pool, garden gnome

      • I run Run for deputy mayor

        Comment it to become a candidate.

        I RUN

      • Deputy Serve 2 episodes

        The most-liked I RUN becomes deputy mayor. The deputy tips every vote and passes one law for the term. Laïa can call a keep-or-repeal vote on that law.

      The ratio veto

      If a reply under a proposal has more likes than the proposal itself, the proposal is cancelled. Owners protect their house the same way. A destroyed house goes back to a basic house that still belongs to the same player.
